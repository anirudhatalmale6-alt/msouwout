About SImACT

Two decades of investing in Haiti, and a platform built for what comes next.

SImACT (Société Immobilière d’Agriculture, de Commerce et de Tourisme) was established in 2004 by a group of Haitian-American professionals and investors united by a shared belief that private investment, diaspora expertise and socially responsible entrepreneurship could contribute meaningfully to Haiti’s economic and social development.

Incorporated in both New York and Haiti, SImACT was created to leverage the knowledge, experience, resources and networks of Haitian-American professionals and investors to participate in Haiti’s emerging investment opportunities.

From its initial group of investors, SImACT has grown its shareholder base and diversified across hospitality, housing, agriculture and mining. Some initiatives have met limited success — the Company’s journey has been shaped by the complex operating environment in Haiti, including recurring political, economic and social disruptions over the past two decades.

Despite these challenges, SImACT has remained committed to its founding vision. Its experience, shareholder base, sectoral interests and knowledge of the Haitian market position it to pursue a new generation of sustainable investment opportunities.

Our Mission

SImACT’s mission is to create sustainable economic value in Haiti through socially conscious investment, enterprise development and strategic partnerships that generate both financial returns and meaningful social impact.

We invest across real estate, agriculture, commerce, tourism and related sectors with the objective of stimulating economic growth, creating productive employment, expanding access to property ownership, strengthening local enterprises and contributing to the revitalization of communities.

Our Vision

To be a leading Haitian investment platform recognized for combining financial performance with sustainable economic and social impact.

We envision a Haiti where strategic investment helps unlock the potential of its people, communities, businesses and natural resources — creating productive opportunities, strengthening local economies, and contributing to a more prosperous and self-reliant nation.

Our Investment Philosophy

Investment performance and economic development can be mutually reinforcing.

Our investors seek more than financial returns. They seek opportunities where capital can generate measurable economic and social value. Our philosophy therefore considers both the commercial viability of an opportunity and its potential contribution to communities and the broader Haitian economy.

We focus on opportunities that:

  • Generate sustainable financial returns
  • Create productive employment and entrepreneurial opportunities
  • Support the development of local businesses and industries
  • Expand access to quality real estate and hospitality services
  • Strengthen agriculture and productive economic activity
  • Encourage tourism and destination development
  • Revitalize underserved communities
  • Contribute to Haiti’s long-term economic resilience and self-reliance

Hospitality & Tourism

Cap Lamandou Hotel

Jacmel, Haiti
Operating asset

Cap Lamandou is one of SImACT’s signature projects in Haiti’s hospitality sector. Located along Jacmel’s scenic Caribbean coastline, the 32-room property represents a longstanding investment in tourism and regional economic development, and reflects our commitment to assets that generate both financial value and economic activity within local communities.

Under the leadership of Dr. Daniel Faustin, the tourism division has worked closely with the hotel’s management to strengthen operations and maintain business continuity despite the significant political, economic and security challenges affecting Haiti’s hospitality industry in recent years.

Market opportunity

Jacmel’s growing appeal as a domestic tourism destination creates demand for comfortable, upscale accommodation. The property’s location, established operations and existing base provide a platform for future growth — including a long-term objective to develop additional private seafront suites, subject to market conditions, feasibility and the availability of appropriate financing.

Investment position

Cap Lamandou forms part of the Company’s broader real estate and tourism portfolio. Continued ownership provides exposure to the long-term potential of Jacmel’s tourism market while preserving the underlying value of the real estate asset.

Real Estate + Hospitality

Belle Rive Residences, Hotel & Club

Jacmel, Haiti
Development opportunity

A planned luxury mixed-use residential and hospitality development on approximately 24 acres fronting the Caribbean Sea, designed to combine upscale accommodation, residential living, leisure and tourism within a distinctive coastal setting.

The development

  • 94 luxury townhouse and tower residences
  • A 120-room, four-star hotel
  • A private residential and social club
  • Boutique retail and service offerings
  • Resort amenities and recreational facilities
  • Access to a private beach minutes from the development

Development vision

Belle Rive is conceived as more than a residential and hotel development. It is intended to create a destination bringing together real estate, tourism, hospitality and local culture — combining contemporary international standards with the cultural identity of Jacmel, with Haitian art, craftsmanship and cuisine incorporated throughout the guest and residential experience.

Economic development potential

The project has the potential to generate employment, stimulate supporting businesses, attract visitors and investment, and expand the availability of high-quality residential and hospitality accommodation in the Jacmel market.

Housing & Community Development

Village Laferrière

Milot, Haiti
Planned · ~500 homes

A proposed affordable housing and community development initiative designed to address the growing need for quality, attainable housing in northern Haiti while creating a model for sustainable community development.

SImACT acquired 191 acres in Milot for a planned private, gated community of approximately 500 homes — designed as an integrated neighbourhood where residents can live, work, socialize and access essential services within a thoughtfully planned environment.

Planned amenities

  • Recreational and sports facilities
  • Community gardens and green spaces
  • Grocery and convenience retail
  • Farmers’ market
  • Community gathering spaces

Making homeownership accessible

SImACT is exploring financing structures that could include subsidized financing and mortgage products designed to make homeownership more attainable for qualified buyers — with the objective of competitive, fixed rates and terms of up to ten years, subject to market conditions, regulatory requirements and agreements with participating financial institutions.

Location & economic opportunity

Milot sits within a region seeing significant infrastructure, institutional, tourism and economic activity — close to Cap-Haïtien International Airport, Milot’s healthcare facilities, the Caracol Industrial Park, educational institutions and major attractions including the Citadelle Laferrière.

Agriculture & Agro-Industry

Agricultural Production & Processing

Haiti
Area of interest

Haiti possesses significant agricultural potential, supported by its agroecological conditions, longstanding farming traditions and experience in food production. SImACT recognizes agriculture as a sector able to generate employment, strengthen local production, improve food security and create opportunities for sustainable economic growth.

Our interest extends beyond primary production to the broader agro-industrial value chain — food processing, value-added production, storage, distribution and related activities — connecting agricultural production with processing and markets.

Mining & Natural Resources

SOMINE

Société Minière du Nord-Est S.A.
Majority shareholding

Haiti possesses substantial, largely underdeveloped mineral resources, including known deposits of gold, copper and other minerals, offering opportunities for responsible exploration and development. SImACT has established a significant presence in the sector through its majority shareholding in Société Minière du Nord-Est S.A. (SOMINE).

SImACT views mining as an opportunity to support economic diversification, job creation, infrastructure development and increased investment in Haiti, while promoting responsible environmental and community practices.
