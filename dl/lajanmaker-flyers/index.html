Ten seconds, before you pay a printer

  1. Open this page on one phone and open the flyer picture.
  2. Point a second phone's camera at the screen.
  3. A link should pop up saying haitibiznis.com. Tap it — the LajanMaker Center page opens.

One phone cannot scan its own screen, which is why you need two.

Two things to fix before you print

Neither is my work — they came with the flyer. I am telling you now because both get expensive once they are on paper.

The big red line says KONKTE

It is missing a letter. Everywhere else — your own badge on the other flyer — it is KONEKTE. It is the largest text on the flyer, so it is the one people will notice.

The address says 123 Avenue Martin Luther King

“123” is the number an AI puts when it does not know the real one. If that is not the true address of the Center, it should be changed or taken off — people will turn up there.

Tell me the right wording and I will put it in.
