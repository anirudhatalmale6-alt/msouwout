Result

Confirmed by SolutionIP (15 Oct)

  • Collecting and paying out are separate transactions, each with its own fee.
  • Payout fees: MonCash 4%, NatCash 3%, KashPaw 3%, per recipient, paid by HaitiBiznis on top.
  • Payouts come from a prefunded payout account; moving money there from the collection account is free.
  • Batch payouts don't reduce fees. No refunds through the integration. No pre-authorisation. No sandbox.

Still unconfirmed: MonCash and NatCash collection %; whether it applies to the full payment; whether the 120-second withdrawal limit affects batches; whether there is an API to read the payout balance.
