1. The fees used

  • Sending money out (paying merchants and Ajan), as quoted to you by SolutionIP: MonCash 4%, NatCash 3%. Their API charges this ON TOP, so HaitiBiznis pays it.
  • Receiving the buyer's payment (collection): not published anywhere. It is not in their API documentation, and their payment confirmations do not show it (I checked your real 20 HTG ticket of 29 Sep). The tables below use 3% as an assumption. Please confirm the real rate with SolutionIP.
  • Delivery is left out: it is a separate charge, 80% to the rider and 20% to HaitiBiznis, which is extra income on top of these figures.

6. What existing merchants agreed to

  • No merchant has a recorded acceptance of any fee. The merchant sign-up page shows no fee and has no "I agree" box.
  • The public Terms (effective 20 Apr 2026, myplopplop.com/terms.html) say merchants accept a 15% commission "by default". The system is set to 10% on 13 shops and 15% on 1. Your policy is 0% during onboarding. The three disagree.
  • No MyPlopPlop order has been paid yet (6 orders, all unpaid), so no merchant has been charged anything.

Recommended before activation: update the Terms to the new pricing; show every merchant a "New pricing" notice in their dashboard with an Accept button (the date and version are recorded); apply the 5% only to sales after that merchant accepts. Until then, 0%.

8. Built and tested (not active)

The merchant's notice. Every merchant sees this in their dashboard, in Kreyòl, French, English or Spanish. Pressing "Mwen dakò" records which version they accepted and when. The 5% applies only to their sales AFTER that.

Each order in the merchant's dashboard: the sale, the seller fee and what they receive. A merchant who has not accepted: 0%, receives everything.

Three stages, switched by you:

  • Off (today): nothing changes.
  • Notice: merchants see the new pricing and can accept. No seller fee for anyone yet (your 0% during onboarding).
  • Active: buyer 7.5%, seller 5% (only merchants who accepted), Ajan 3% of the products.

Tested on a test copy: 25 server checks, 17 screen checks, 12 checkout checks. One example: a merchant who accepted sells 1,000. The buyer pays 1,225 with delivery, the merchant receives 950, the Ajan 30. A merchant who has not accepted: 0%. A buyer paying less than the order is refused.

9. Terms: proposed new wording (for your approval)

Section 12, Fees:

  • Registering a business and listing products on MyPlopPlop are free.
  • Seller transaction fee: 5% of the eligible product sale amount, deducted from the merchant's settlement only after the order is completed and the payment is confirmed. It applies only to sales made after the merchant has accepted the current pricing in the merchant dashboard. The dashboard shows the fee and the expected net settlement on every order.
  • Buyer platform fee: 7.5% of the product amount, paid by the buyer and shown before checkout. It is never taken from the merchant.

Section 10, Merchant Terms: the old "commission (default 15%)" is replaced by "the seller transaction fee set out in section 12".

Section 8, Referral: the old "10% of platform fees for the first 3 months" is replaced by: Ajan earn 3% of the eligible amount (MyPlopPlop products, MsouWout completed and paid fares to the driver's Ajan, Tikè Lakay ticket price), for 12 months. Paid from the platform's own fees, never from the merchant, driver or buyer. No commission on cancelled or refunded sales. Enterprise Ajan are credited to their Enterprise, never paid twice.
