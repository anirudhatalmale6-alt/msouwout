Sponsor Commission Structure

HaitiBiznis Koutye (Ajan) system. Written 15 Oct 2026 from your referral PDF and your decisions of 15 Oct.
STATUS: DOCUMENTED ONLY. NOT ACTIVE. No sponsor commission is calculated or paid anywhere today. Nothing here is switched on without your written approval.

1. The two levels

  • Level 1, the direct Ajan: the Ajan whose code brought the customer, driver or business. Earns the platform's normal rate.
  • Level 2, the sponsor: the Ajan who recruited that Level 1 Ajan. Earns 20% of the Level 1 commission. Not another full percentage.
  • Only these two levels are paid. There is no level 3.

4. Rules from your PDF

  1. The sponsor is paid from the platform's referral budget. It must NEVER reduce the driver's payout, the DASH allocation, or the direct Ajan's commission.
  2. The first valid sponsor is locked. Another code or QR cannot take an existing Ajan.
  3. 12-month attribution, the same as direct referrals.
  4. No self-referral and no circular referral (A sponsors B, B sponsors A).
  5. Same engine for every platform. Commission only on completed, paid sales; cancelled or refunded sales reverse both levels.

5. What is not built yet

  • No "sponsor" link between Ajan exists in the system. Each Ajan would need a locked sponsor field, set once when they register with a sponsor's code.
  • No Level 2 calculation, no Level 2 line in the Ajan's wallet or dashboard, no reversal for Level 2.
