If you download it twice, your phone renames the second copy to "msouwout-115.aab (1)" — and Google Play then says "Import a valid app bundle", because the name no longer ends in .aab. If you see that, delete the copies from your Downloads and tap once.

How to upload it

  1. Open Play Console and choose MsouWout.
  2. Go to Production, then Create new release.
  3. Tap Upload and pick the file you just downloaded.
  4. For "What's new", write: Faster loading and up-to-date prices.
  5. Tap Next, then Save.
  6. Tap Send for review.
  7. Google reviews it. Usually a day or two.
