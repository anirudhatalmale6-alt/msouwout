HaitiBiznis Ecosystem: Security Audit

16 Oct 2026, all free. Covers MyPlopPlop, MsouWout, Tikè Lakay / HaitiBiznis / LajanMaker, 48HoursReady and StarFlex. Every item was checked in the live code, and every fix was tested (and shown to fail on the old code) before deploy. Fixed live and verified · Open still to do · Decision needs you

The class you were worried about, a small payment confirming a big purchase, existed in 3 more places. All three are closed:

  • MsouWout rides;
  • Tikè Lakay tickets and courses;
  • MyPlopPlop electricity/water bills.

Every MonCash/NatCash confirmation now also checks the amount paid, not just "ok".

1. Payment amounts and fake payments

  • FixedMsouWout: a ride could be booked with any price the phone sent (20 HTG for any trip). Now refused under the real fare.
  • FixedMsouWout: MonCash/NatCash "ok" was accepted for any amount. Now the amount paid must equal the charge.
  • FixedTikè Lakay and courses: same amount check.
  • FixedMyPlopPlop bills (EDH/DINEPA): a 20 HTG payment marked a bill paid. Also, "500" typed as text made a 50,025 HTG charge.
  • FixedMsouWout: partial payments, the same payment confirmed twice, a retry after a failed page, and a payment arriving after cancellation. Each is handled once, and any extra money is recorded as owed back, never as income.
  • FixedTikè Lakay: a ticket quantity of "-50" reopened 50 seats. Quantity is now 1 to 20.

2. Administrator access

  • FixedMsouWout and HaitiBiznis: typing an admin address in CAPITAL letters skipped the password. That exposed driver payouts and bank details, all orders, and POS licence grants.
  • FixedMsouWout: the admin password was still the original default, written in the public code. It is retired (the old one no longer works, checked on live) and now stored only as an encrypted hash, with a lockout after 8 wrong tries.
  • FixedTwo-factor login with an authenticator app (Google/Microsoft Authenticator). Once you set it up, the password alone opens nothing.
  • FixedPasswords and secrets removed from all code. All past versions were checked: no database, payment, email or API key was ever in the code. The old console PIN from early versions no longer works.
  • FixedHaitiBiznis: the WhatsApp bot "test" door let anyone act as any phone number. It is now admin-only. The paid AI flyer scan now has a limit of 10 per hour.

3. Money moving twice, or without payment

  • FixedPayouts locked on MsouWout, as you asked: payout runs, approvals, bank and MonCash/NatCash changes. They stay locked until you say so. Automatic payouts are off.
  • FixedDouble-tap duplicates closed: MyPlopPlop driver withdrawals, agent (Ajan) payout requests and "mark paid", sol group payouts, featured boosts, and refund records on all platforms.
  • FixedMyPlopPlop: a negative boost length or negative sol fee added money to a wallet. Sol payouts paid out money the app never received. Agent "package" commissions were created before any payment. All closed.
  • FixedCommissions before payment: the console now refuses to approve a package commission with no completed payment. The old pre-payment referral bonus was switched off on 15 Oct.
  • FixedMsouWout: anyone with a passenger's phone number could delete her account, and that erased drivers' earnings on her rides. Money rides are now kept (her personal details removed), and a ride in progress cannot be deleted.
  • FixedRefunds owed are visible: MsouWout now lists every refund owed on the money page's "Needs a person" box, with a button to record the transfer reference once. MyPlopPlop has its "Swivi" page. 2 real MsouWout refunds were owed and never shown: 318 HTG (23 Sep) and 284 HTG (10 Oct), same customer. Please check and send them, then record the reference.

4. Customer information

  • FixedOn 15 Oct: driver names, phones and ID-photo paths, electricity tokens, and ride details readable by anyone.
  • FixedMyPlopPlop: live chat, driver positions and new-ride addresses were broadcast to anyone connected. Delivery codes leaked to drivers and shops.
  • OpenTikè Lakay: ticket references can be listed by phone number, and the rate limit is fixed. Fully closing it needs a code sent to the buyer's phone.

Still open

  1. DecisionMsouWout: a passenger can cancel even after the ride has started (cancellation is free, so she gets a full refund and the driver gets nothing), or press "emergency" to freeze it. Block cancel once started? Your call; changing it changes the app.
  2. OpenMsouWout: deleting a DRIVER account needs only his phone number. Fix: ask for his PIN (a small page change).
  3. OpenMsouWout: extra-passenger fees are not added to the server-side price, so a modified page could skip them. Low money impact.
  4. OpenTikè Lakay: seats are counted when paid, so the last seats can be oversold if many people pay at once. Needs seat reservations.
  5. OpenTikè Lakay: a buyer can enter their own agent code and get the 3% back on their own tickets. Policy question: allow it or not?
  6. OpenWhatsApp bot: messages from Meta are not signature-checked. Needs the Meta "app secret" from your WhatsApp account.
  7. OpenMyPlopPlop: about 20 lower-risk items (status changes that could be clicked twice in old admin screens, sol and international-order edge cases). None moves money without an administrator.
  8. Open48HoursReady API: serious holes (no admin login, commissions without payment), but it is not deployed, so nothing is reachable. It must be fixed before it goes live.
  9. DecisionStarFlex (owned by your partner, so I changed nothing): the login code is sent back in the server's reply. Anyone could take over a citizen account. No money is involved. Recommendation for the owner: connect an SMS/email sender and turn the demo mode off.
  10. OpenCode repositories are still public. Nothing secret is in them. Making them private needs your Render workspace, whenever you want.

Administrator access logs (MsouWout)

What exists: Render keeps the MsouWout server log for about 7 days. I searched all of it: 9 Oct 04:19 to 16 Oct 04:19 UTC, 51,112 lines and 2,965 requests to admin addresses.

  • CleanThe capital-letter hole was used only 3 times, all by me (00:58 UTC today, while proving it), before it was closed.
  • CleanEvery answered request for payouts, the money report, the driver list and the full ride list came from my own test tools. No unknown phone or computer read them.
  • CleanThe only other answered calls were normal app use: a driver opening his own earnings page, the public clinic list, and two sign-ups made by my tests.
  • UnknownBefore 9 October there are no logs, so I cannot prove whether anyone used the old default password earlier. It was in public code. That is why it was treated as compromised and retired.

The search runs inside the deploy system. The matching lines are encrypted with a key only I hold, and nothing personal is printed anywhere public.

Unresolved financial risks

  • Refunds are sent by hand (SolutionIP cannot refund), so a person must clear the "owed" list.
  • Payouts are manual and currently locked. Earnings are recorded but not sent.
  • MyPlopPlop 2-shop baskets: the second shop's delivery needs an admin to set the driver's pay, until the multi-shop feature exists.
  • No real-money test yet on the new code (planned with Jennifer).
