Payout Engine & Financial Dashboard: What Is Still Needed

For MsouWout, MyPlopPlop, Tikè Lakay and 48HoursReady, which all use the same SolutionIP account. Written 15 Oct 2026. Each item says why it matters and what happens without it.

A. Questions for SolutionIP (Guy)

  1. 1. Collection fee: MonCash %, NatCash %, KashPaw %. Is it charged on the full amount the customer pays (delivery included)? Any fixed fee or minimum per payment? Without it, every "net earnings" figure is an estimate.
  2. 2. An API to read the payout account balance (and the collection account balance, if possible). Their documentation has none. Without it the dashboard can only show a running estimate (money moved in, minus payouts and fees), not the real balance.
  3. 3. The 120-second wait between withdrawals. Does it apply to every payout, including a batch? Can it be lifted for our account? If it stays: one payout every 2 minutes. 50 drivers ≈ 1 hour 40 minutes per payout run.
  4. 4. Webhook address. The 4 products share one client ID. Can each product have its own client ID, or must one webhook address receive everything? Decides how payment confirmations are routed to the right product.
  5. 5. KashPaw payouts. Their documentation lists only MonCash and NatCash for payouts. What is the method code for KashPaw? A driver or merchant who only has KashPaw cannot be paid otherwise.
  6. 6. Payout limits. Minimum and maximum per payout, daily limit, any fixed fee besides the 3–4%.
  7. 7. A failed payout. When the status is "remboursé", how long until the money is back in the payout balance?
  8. 8. A statement or export of collections and payouts by date. Needed to reconcile our records with theirs every day.
  9. 9. Recipient check. Can we see the name on a MonCash/NatCash number before sending? Protects against sending money to a mistyped number.
  10. 10. The client secret. Not for me: you enter it yourself in the Render settings. It must never be sent in chat or WhatsApp. Required to sign payouts and to verify webhook messages.

B. Decisions for HaitiBiznis

  1. 1. Who pays the 3–4% payout fee? The recommendation, matching your instruction: HaitiBiznis pays it on top, so the driver gets the full 80% and the merchant the full settlement.
  2. 2. Payout schedule. Daily or weekly? Minimum amount per payout? Merchants already have a hold of 24 hours (72 hours for a new shop).
  3. 3. Who approves each payout run before money moves. One administrator, or two?
  4. 4. Funding the payout account. Who moves money from the collection account, and at what balance should the system warn you?
  5. 5. Payout numbers. Every driver, merchant and Ajan must register the MonCash/NatCash number to be paid on, which is not always their login phone. DASH currently has a bank reference, not a wallet number.
  6. 6. Refunds. SolutionIP cannot refund, so a refund is a manual payout, and it costs 3–4% too. Who bears that fee? Who approves a refund, and within how many days?

C. Already built and reused (no rebuild)

  1. Collecting payments through SolutionIP, with server-side verification and unique references.
  2. Who is owed what: MsouWout records each ride's driver, DASH and platform amounts; MyPlopPlop records each order's merchant settlement, driver 80% and platform 20%.
  3. The Ajan commission ledger, with the Enterprise rule (no double payment) and reversals for refunds (built, waiting for your approval).
  4. The profit simulator, for planning: msouwout.com/dl/mpp-pricing/simulator.html
