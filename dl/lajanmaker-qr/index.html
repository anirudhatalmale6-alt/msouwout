For the video

Two things that decide whether people can scan it

  1. Leave it on screen for at least 4 seconds. A phone camera needs a second or two to find the code, and the viewer needs time to pick their phone up.
  2. Keep it big. The code should take up at least a third of the height of the screen. It was tested down to a 480p export with heavy blur and it still read, but small and blurry together is what kills it.
