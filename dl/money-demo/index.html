3. A StarFlex Ajan

  • MsouWout ride of 1,000: 30, credited to STARFLEX only.
  • MyPlopPlop order of 1,000 from a StarFlex Ajan's shop: 30, credited to STARFLEX only.
  • The Ajan's own wallet: 0. They cannot request a payout of StarFlex's money.
  • StarFlex's dashboard total rises by exactly 30. Payouts shown as not active.

4. Cancellations and refunds

  • Refunded ride: the commission line becomes "reversed" (kept with the reason and date, never deleted). The Ajan's balance drops back by 15.
  • Reversed twice: nothing more is taken.
  • StarFlex refunded ride: StarFlex's total falls back by 30.
  • A commission already PAID is never silently erased. It is flagged for you to recover.
  • A cancelled ride never earned a commission in the first place.

5. Balances

  • Independent Ajan: +15 when the ride is paid, back to the start after the refund.
  • Every figure comes from the commission lines themselves, so the wallet, the dashboard and StarFlex's total always agree.

6. Still needed from you before switching on

  • MonCash/NatCash receiving rate (the % kept when a customer pays). After the Ajan's 30 and a 40 MonCash payout to the merchant, a 1,000 order leaves about 15 HTG BEFORE that rate.
  • Merchants 0%: OK to set all 14 stores from 10% to 0%?
  • Old duplicate bonuses OFF: the order "referral bonus" (paid when an order is created, before payment) and the old driver-refers-driver "ambassador bonus". Neither has ever paid anyone.
  • MsouWout start date: the first day rides earn the 3%. No back-pay before it.

7. Also fixed: checkout charges the server's total

MyPlopPlop used to send MonCash the total worked out in the buyer's phone. Now it charges the total the server calculated. If that differs from what the buyer saw, the buyer is asked "Pay X HTG?" before any money moves. Tested: buyer says no, nothing is sent; buyer says yes, the server's total is charged.
