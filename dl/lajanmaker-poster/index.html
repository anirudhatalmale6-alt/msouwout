One thing about the size you asked for

You asked for 30″ × 40″. That is a tall shape, and this artwork is a wide one — twice as wide as it is tall. It cannot fill a 30×40 sheet without stretching the faces or cutting off the sides.

So this file is 40″ wide × 20″ tall, which is the artwork's own shape at the same 40 inch length. If you want a tall 30×40 for the wall, tell me and I will lay it out properly — no charge.

Give the printer this

  1. Print at 40 inches wide by 20 inches tall.
  2. Do not crop, stretch or “fit to page”. The QR must stay square.
  3. Matte or satin finish. A glossy finish reflects the room lights and can stop a phone reading the code.
