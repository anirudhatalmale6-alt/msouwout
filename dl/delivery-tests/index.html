1. Payment security

  • PassedA shop cannot accept, prepare or mark ready an order that is not paid. No driver is ever sent for an unpaid order.
  • PassedAn order is "paid" only when the server confirms it with SolutionIP. A payment smaller than the order is refused.
  • PassedA basket from 2 shops = 1 payment, for the server's exact total. That one payment confirms both shops' orders. Paying only one shop's amount confirms neither.
  • PassedThe customer paid, then closed the page or lost connection: the server checks SolutionIP on its own at 2, 5, 10, 20 and 40 minutes, then every hour for 24 hours. Checking again never charges or confirms twice.
  • PassedStill unpaid after 24 hours: the order is cancelled and its stock goes back on the shelf.
  • FixedOrders from before today are left as they are. Without this, the automatic check could have marked months-old orders paid and paid agent commission on them. A test proves it.

2. Driver assignment

  • PassedTwo drivers press "Accept" at the same instant: exactly one gets the delivery.
  • PassedOnly approved drivers whose position is less than 15 minutes old are offered jobs. The driver app now sends the position every minute.
  • PassedA driver drops the delivery: it goes back to searching and is offered to another driver, never back to him. The customer is told.
  • PassedNo driver for 10 minutes: the customer, the shop and all admins are alerted once, and it appears on the admin's new "Swivi" page.
  • FixedA delivery with 0 HTG for the driver is never offered. That happens with a shop's "free delivery", or the 2nd shop of a basket, because the trip is charged once. An admin sets the driver's pay with a written reason.

3. Pickup and delivery proof

  • PassedPickup: the driver who accepted gets a 4-digit pickup code. The shop types it to hand over the parcel. A wrong code, another shop, or the driver alone cannot do it. The time and the driver's position are recorded.
  • PassedDelivery: the delivery code is shown only on the customer's order page, with a warning. The shop never sees it, and neither does the driver: not in offers, the jobs list or his own list.
  • PassedThe driver must type the customer's code at the door. After 5 wrong codes, even the right one is refused.
  • PassedAdmin exception (customer lost their phone, etc.): admin only, a written reason is required, and who and why are recorded. A shop cannot use it.
  • PassedNo route can mark a delivery "delivered" without the code. The shop's status button refuses. An old side-door that booked a separate MsouWout cash ride with no code is closed.
  • PassedA customer who collects at the shop gives the shop their code.

4. Driver earnings: exactly 80%, exactly once

  • PassedThe driver is credited exactly 80% of the delivery fee the customer paid. Examples: 200 of 250 in one test, 128 of 160 in the browser test. He sees the amount before accepting.
  • Passed"Delivered" sent twice at the same moment (a bad connection) and again later: credited once. The shop's settlement is also credited once (2,000 − 10% = 1,800).
  • PassedEarnings are held (driver 24 h, shop 24 h or 72 h for a new shop), then become withdrawable once. Running the release again moves nothing.
  • FixedWithdrawal requests: two quick taps used to create two withdrawals of the same balance. Rejecting twice gave the money back twice. Both now happen once only, and "sent" needs the MonCash/NatCash reference.

5. Cancellations and refunds

  • PassedWho can cancel when:
    • the customer, until the shop starts preparing;
    • the shop, until the handover (a reason is required);
    • an admin, until delivery.
    A delivered order cannot be cancelled: it becomes a refund case.
  • PassedOn cancel, the delivery is stopped, the stock comes back and the agent's commission is reversed. If the order was paid, a refund opens automatically.
  • PassedA cancelled order can never be delivered and pays nobody, even if the driver didn't hear the news.
  • PassedRefund steps, all on the "Swivi" page:
    1. An admin approves it.
    2. The page says "Send X HTG by MonCash to (phone)".
    3. The admin types the transfer reference.
    Every step is kept in its history. A shop cannot approve refunds.
  • PassedA refund after delivery (e.g. a damaged item, 500 HTG) is taken back from the shop's held balance when it is approved.

6. Security and privacy

  • PassedEvery new admin action is admin-only on the server. Strangers cannot read anyone's order. Checked on the live server after the update: all 10 new routes refuse visitors who are not logged in.
  • FixedLive chat and driver position: until today anyone, even without logging in, could do three things.
    • Listen to any delivery's chat and the driver's live position.
    • Post fake messages or a fake position.
    • Receive the addresses of every new ride.
    Now only that ride's customer and driver can; new jobs go to approved drivers only. Checked on the live server.
  • FixedA driver could read the customer's delivery code from his own ride list. This was live until today.
  • FixedA shop could have read the driver's pickup code. My own test caught this before release.
  • PassedNo secret payment key is in any page. Only SolutionIP's client ID is there, and it is meant to be public. No customer phone numbers or addresses are on public pages (fixed last night).
  • FixedPages:
    • The tracking page always said "Ride Not Found"; it works now.
    • The driver's screen erased the customer's code while he was typing it.
    • The customer's page showed the shop's commission as a "Platform fee".
    All corrected.

Remaining limitations

  1. LimitNo real money was used. SolutionIP has no test mode, so payments were tested with a stand-in that answers like SolutionIP. One small controlled real payment is still needed (see below).
  2. LimitRefunds and payouts are sent by hand. SolutionIP cannot refund, and you asked to keep payouts manual. The app tracks what is owed and records each transfer reference.
  3. Limit2-shop baskets: the delivery is charged once, so each extra shop's parcel waits for an admin to set the driver's pay, which HaitiBiznis pays. Your decision:
    • (a) charge delivery per shop, or
    • (b) one driver collects from both shops (a new feature).
  4. LimitThe delivery distance starts from central Port-au-Prince when a shop has no GPS location. Shops are never asked for one yet (from the audit).
  5. LimitThere is no live map for the customer yet. Alerts are app/browser notifications only, with no SMS or WhatsApp. These were "after launch" items.
  6. LimitAlso from the audit, outside these 5 areas: driver ID/photo upload and payout number, a "suspend driver" button, and the admin console code in the web address.
