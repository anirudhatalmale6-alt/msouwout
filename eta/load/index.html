MsouWout load test — the report

Run against the live system on 14 September 2026, with your go-ahead.

It failed the first time, and it found a real bug. Out of 80 passengers ordering in the same instant, 19 orders were lost — the passenger got an error and no ride existed. I found the cause, fixed it, deployed it, and ran the whole thing again: 80 out of 80, nothing lost.

What was wrong

One line, and it only shows itself under load.

Every ride gets a tracking code like MW-4821, and the database refuses two rides with the same code. That code was built from the clock — down to the millisecond. Two passengers ordering in the same millisecond got the same code, so the second one was rejected by the database and her order vanished with a server error.

That is why it never showed up in normal testing. With two or three phones nobody lands on the same millisecond. With eighty, many do.

Cleanup

30 test drivers and 80 test passengers were created and then deleted. The driver list is back to the same four it held before — Fabrice, Eliassaint, your own account and the demo account — and the ride board is empty. Nothing of mine is left in your system.

Before starting I checked that no real driver was online and no real passenger was waiting, and the test was written to abort if either was true. Nobody was shown a fake ride.

One thing to know for later, not a fault A driver's screen shows at most 20 waiting rides at a time. With 80 waiting that is fine — he takes one and the next appears. But if you ever have more than 20 waiting in one area, drivers will not see past the first 20. Worth revisiting when volume grows; it does not need changing now.

Recommendations before launch

  • Re-run this after any change to ordering or dispatch. The test is written and takes about 15 seconds. This bug survived weeks of two-phone testing.
  • Do the same for the money paths once ride payments are built — a payment that collides under load is worse than a ride that does.
  • The 20-ride board cap above, when you pass that kind of volume.
  • Still outstanding from the inventory: no SMS/WhatsApp/push, no in-app payment, no zone filtering, and the driver login still does not check the PIN. None of those are load problems; they are on the main list.
